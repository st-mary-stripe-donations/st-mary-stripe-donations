St. Mary Church's Ongoing Support for the Children of La Gonave

The children of the Parish of Sainte Madeleine in La Gonâve, Haiti, are facing a serious crisis. More than 600 children in this small, remote community struggle with access to education and adequate nutrition.

Fund Information

Standing With the Children of La Gonâve

The Parish of Sainte Madeleine is a lifeline to its community, striving to meet the needs of its children despite a very difficult situation.

The fund page highlights more than 600 children whose lives are affected by limited access to basic education and nutrition.

St. Mary Church in Norwich continues to support the children of La Gonâve in a spirit of Catholic solidarity, charity, and social justice.

St. Mary Church's Ongoing Support

The children of Sainte Madeleine are not simply names or statistics. They belong to a faith community whose needs have become part of St. Mary Church's continuing concern and outreach.

By supporting this fund, parishioners and friends help keep attention focused on the children of La Gonâve and the urgent needs described by the parish fund.
